Privacy Policy — Medisole
Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview
Medisole ("the App") is a gait analysis application that connects to Bluetooth-enabled insole sensors to monitor foot pressure in real time. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect
Data you provide:

Name, age, and body weight — entered during onboarding and used solely to personalise gait analysis results within the App.
Data generated by the App:

Foot pressure sensor readings from connected Bluetooth insole devices (raw ADC values from 5 sensors per foot).
Gait classification results (e.g. normal, Parkinson's-associated gait patterns) produced locally or by a configured analysis server.
Session history stored locally on your device.
CSV recordings of bilateral pressure frames, exported manually by the user.
Data we do NOT collect:

Location data.
Contacts, photos, or any other device content.
Advertising identifiers.
Any data transmitted to third-party analytics or advertising services.
3. How We Use Your Data
All data collected by Medisole is used exclusively to:

Display real-time pressure heatmaps and gait metrics within the App.
Produce gait classification results for research and clinical support purposes.
Allow you to export recordings for your own research or medical records.
We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

4. Data Storage and Sharing
All personal and sensor data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database (SQLite).
If you configure the App to use an external analysis server (Python backend), sensor window data is transmitted to that server over your local network. This server is operated by you or your institution — we do not operate or have access to it.
Exported CSV files are shared only through your device's standard share sheet, to destinations you choose.
We do not transmit any data to Medisole servers, cloud services, or any third party.
5. Bluetooth
The App uses Bluetooth solely to communicate with paired insole sensor devices. No Bluetooth data is shared with third parties or stored beyond the current session's sensor readings.

6. Children's Privacy
Medisole is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Retention and Deletion
All data is stored on your device and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the App. We have no access to your data and cannot delete it on your behalf.

8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
